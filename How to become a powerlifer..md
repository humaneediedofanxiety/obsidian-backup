The [[The four rules to become a champ.|four rules to become a champ]] is as follows, **do not be afraid to lift heavy, focus on your technique, accumulate a good amount of volume and set clear structured goals** within a specific timeframe.

**When executing the [[Get a stronger bench press with this technique.|bench press]]**, start by planting your feet firmly and using your legs to generate power, as if you're pushing the floor away. Lie back with your chest up and [[How to bench press without shoulder pain.|squeeze your shoulder blades together and downward]], creating a stable base. Unrack the bar with a slight extension of your elbows, then control the descent by letting the bar sink naturally towards your chest while keeping your shoulders retracted. As you press, drive up steadily and diagonally. 

The best accessories for the lift, **close-grip bench press,** **weighted dips**, **skull crushers** or **pushdowns** for pure tricep work, **overhead press** and **face pulls** for shoulder work.

**When executing the [[The ultimate deadlift technique for strength.|conventional deadlift]]**, stand with your feet hip-width apart so the bar sits over the middle of your feet, and as you bend to grab it,[[The best tips to get a stronger deadlift.| push your hips back]] while keeping your back flat—think of it like sitting back into a chair. Before you lift, squeeze your shoulder blades together and pull the bar close to your shins, removing any slack. Then, drive through your heels by simultaneously pushing with your legs and hips in a smooth, coordinated motion until you’re fully upright. Throughout the lift, keep your core tight to protect your lower back, making the movement both efficient and safe.

The best accessories for the lift, **pullups to strengthen the lats**, **deficit deadlifts**, **romanian deadlifts**, **hip thrusts** to strengthen glutes, **hamstring curls** to strengthen posterior chain, **rack pulls** to help with the lockout and finally **heavy holds** to[[Improve grip strength for a better deadlift.| improve grip]].

**When executing the [[How to blow up your squat with this technique.|low-bar squat]]**, set up with feet shoulder-width apart and a slight outward toe angle for stability, ensuring the barbell rests comfortably across your posterior deltoids. Brace your core tightly before descending, and maintain a stable torso angle as you flex at both the knees and hips. Focus on keeping a tripod foot contact—pressure on the heel, base of the big toe, and little toe—to enhance balance and force transfer. On the ascent, drive powerfully through your heels while engaging your glutes and quads, resulting in a controlled and efficient lift. 

The best accessories for the lifts, **pause squats** and **tempo squats** for control, **front squats** for quad strength or even **leg-press**, **bulgarian lunges** for glute strength.

The current program includes **three weekly sessions**: the first focuses on sets of five for volume at 75% of your one-rep max, the second on sets of three for heavier weight at 85% of your one-rep max, and the third on singles, starting at 90% of your one-rep max and increasing.
