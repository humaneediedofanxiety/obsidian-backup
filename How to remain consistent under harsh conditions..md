And how do I remain consistent even when things are not the best, there are a very few basic points to keep in mind and those are all that is required, the first point is to **keep everything easy to start**, choose only a few things to stick to at once and keep the tasks easy to do and add options to do more once the bare minimum is done, the second point is **keep track of everything**, accountability is important and keeping track of your progress also works as a push to do more and finally **keep things fun, intentional and allow room for flexibility** and always review your goals and modify your habits accordingly.
