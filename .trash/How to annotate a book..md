The process of annotation really makes me feel like I own the book and increases the satisfaction of reading for me and that is exactly why I do it and not for the purpose of retaining any information later on, those are side effects.

The process, simply get a **pen and a highlighter** if you like and that is all you will ever need, maybe some **sticky tabs** or notes too but that is not necessary. For fiction books, when you really find a paragraph or a sentence that really resonates with you, mark them either with a pen or a highlighter and write down short-thoughts on either the margins or use sticky tabs if there is not enough space.

The process of reading shouldn't be that complicated, get a book and a pen and start reading, note things that you really liked and write down thoughts you had and that is everything you need to do.
